# Learning Photography & Videography

A self-contained guide to photography/videography fundamentals — what the
numbers on a camera and lens mean, and how to compose a shot once you've
got them set.

## What's here

### Camera & Lens Numbers

A guide to what all the numbers on a camera and lens actually mean
(`28-75mm`, `F/2.8`, ISO, stops, and so on) — built from two custom pieces
plus a curated list of existing tools that cover the same ground.

- **[CameraLensNumbers.mp4](CameraLensNumbers.mp4)** (Part 1) — a ~2
  minute narrated-style animation decoding a real lens (Tamron `28-75mm
  F/2.8 Di III VXD G2`) using a "bucket left out in the rain" analogy for
  aperture, shutter speed and ISO. Source/rebuild instructions:
  [../camera_lens_numbers/](../camera_lens_numbers/).
- **[CameraLensNumbersV2.mp4](CameraLensNumbersV2.mp4)** (Part 2) — the
  follow-up questions Part 1 glosses over: what focal length physically
  *is* (not a ruler-length of glass), why it controls magnification, why
  a "constant" aperture means the diameter grows rather than the ratio,
  the actual cone-of-light/circle-of-confusion reason a wide aperture
  blurs backgrounds, and why that still works when the sensor is a whole
  area rather than a single point.
- **[game.html](game.html)** — an interactive browser quiz testing the
  Part 1 material (which aperture is physically bigger, balancing the
  exposure triangle, decoding a real lens name). Just open the file in
  any browser, no install needed.

### Composition

- **[CompositionRules.mp4](CompositionRules.mp4)** — seven core
  composition ideas, each paired with a small original vector scene:
  texture & repetition, curving lines, foreground, rule of thirds,
  vanishing point, frame within a frame, and center. Source/rebuild
  instructions: [../composition_rules/](../composition_rules/).

## More resources

Existing tools that teach the same concepts, for comparison or extra
practice — none of them do the "decode a real lens name" or scored-quiz
part, which is what the game above adds.

**A real game (Steam):**
- [Photography Simulator](https://store.steampowered.com/app/1732910/Photography_Simulator/)
  — a wildlife-photography game where you manually adjust zoom, aperture,
  focus and shutter speed to get the shot. Gameplay-first (stalking
  animals, building a following), but the exposure controls are real.

**Free web simulators** (sliders + live visual feedback, closer to a
teaching tool):
- [Exposure Triangle Explorer](https://exposure-triangle.arnebaeyens.com/)
  — drag to balance aperture/shutter/ISO at the correct exposure, watch
  depth of field, motion blur and noise trade off across sensor sizes.
- [Tools for Film's Exposure Triangle Simulator](https://www.toolsforfilm.com/tools/exposure-triangle)
  — the same idea, aimed at filmmakers.
- [CameraSimulator.online](https://www.camerasimulator.online/) — five
  animated scenes, live optical results as you turn each dial.
- [dima.fi Exposure Triangle simulator](https://dima.fi/exposure/) — a
  simpler, older version of the same concept.
